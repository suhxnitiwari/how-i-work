Relationship Building

With four of my five strengths in Relationship Building, people shape how I work. I notice how someone likes to communicate, what they light up talking about, what they’re quietly great at, and when they could use a little more encouragement than usual. I like figuring out what makes each person them, and making sure those differences have a place on the team.

Influencing

I have a lot of thoughts, and fortunately, I usually have the words for them too. I love taking an idea and figuring out how to make someone else care about it: through a story, a presentation, a pitch, or the exact sentence that finally makes it click. I’m comfortable speaking up, asking the question everyone’s thinking, and giving an idea enough energy that other people want to join in.

How it all works together

My strengths work as one loop: I notice the person first, then shape the message for the person actually hearing it. That’s how noticing turns into thoughtful feedback, genuine encouragement, or a pitch that lands. It also means I can be direct and decisive while still paying attention to the people involved. The goal isn’t just to be heard. It’s to make an idea understood, memorable, and worth responding to, because working well with people starts with actually seeing them.
